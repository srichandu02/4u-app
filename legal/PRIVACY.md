## 1. What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | Account sign-in (OTP), account recovery |
| Display name, bio, interests, age, city | Shown to your matches to enable connection |
| Messages, call metadata (duration, type) | Delivering chat and call history between matched users |
| Reports you file | Trust & safety review |
| Device/browser info (via hosting provider logs) | Security, abuse prevention |

We do **not** collect your precise location, government ID, or payment
card details directly — coin purchases, if enabled, are processed by
[PAYMENT PROVIDER], who handles your card data under their own privacy
policy.

## 2. How we use it

- To operate matchmaking, chat, and calls
- To keep the platform safe (reviewing reports, preventing abuse)
- To communicate with you about your account (not marketing, unless you
  opt in)

We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. What your matches can see

When you connect with someone, they can see your display name, age, city,
bio, interests, and any messages you send them. They cannot see your email
address.

## 4. Data storage & security

Your data is stored with [Supabase / your database provider], using
row-level security so users can only access their own data and data
belonging to matches they're part of. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

## 5. Data retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. If you delete
your account, your profile, messages, and match history are permanently
deleted within [30 days], except where we're required to retain reports
for safety/legal purposes.

## 6. Your rights

- **Access** — you can view your profile data in-app at any time
- **Correction** — edit your profile directly in-app
- **Deletion** — Profile → Delete my account, or email [SUPPORT EMAIL]
- **Portability** — email [SUPPORT EMAIL] to request an export of your data

If you're in the EU/UK/California, you may have additional rights under
GDPR/CCPA — contact us to exercise them.

## 7. Children's privacy

4U is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data
from minors. If we learn a user is under 18, we terminate the account and
delete their data.
